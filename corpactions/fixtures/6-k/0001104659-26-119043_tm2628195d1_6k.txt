PRICE SENSITIVE Eni: results for the third quarter and nine months of 2026 3Q operational and financial results support further upgrade to FY '26 outlook with record low debt and enhanced shareholders' returns through extraordinary dividend and increased buyback 3Q '26 performance reflects the strength of our strategy, continued execution and disciplined investment into high-quality opportunities. Considering the excellent operating and financial performance of the nine months '26 and an improved cash flow projection for the '26 FY (€16.5 bln), Eni has resolved to distribute an extraordinary dividend of €0.46 per sh. (ca. €1.3 bln) in December and a further increase of 12% in the buyback program to €3.8 bln. Underlying E&P production up 7% to 1.83 mln boe/d driven by projects ramp-ups and full quarter contribution from Searah JV. A strategic contract to relaunch production at the giant Junin 5 field in Venezuela has been signed, materially enhancing future upstream growth options. Plenitude deconsolidation creates an efficient platform for acceleration in the next phase of growth. Dual exploration sale of 10% stake in Baleine project and monetization of infrastructure portfolio reflect efficient capital management. Binding agreements signed to farm out retained 10% stake in the Kutei basin in Indonesia. Strong cash generation and disciplined capital allocation reduce proforma gearing to historic low of 9%, reinforcing resilience and capacity to invest for growth. Rome, October 23, 2026 - Eni's Board of Directors, chaired by Giuseppina Di Foggia, yesterday approved the consolidated results for the third quarter and nine months of 2026. Eni CEO Claudio Descalzi said: "Eni's excellent results in 3Q were driven by the strength of our operating model and disciplined investments into a well-diversified portfolio of advantaged assets. Due to our strong fundamentals, continuously improving growth prospects, and a historically robust financial performance, we are well positioned to continue delivering to our customers affordable, secure and increasingly decarbonized supplies. In 3Q, E&P again delivered outstanding underlying production growth of 7%, reflecting project ramp-ups and full operativity of our assets and the benefit of the start-up of our Searah JV in Southeast Asia. Moreover, during this quarter we have fast tracked a new wave of material initiatives in South America, Africa and Southeast Asia, which will enhance growth and cash generation for years to come. Finally, thanks to our outstanding exploration activities, this year we have discovered 1.23 bln barrels of new hydrocarbon resources, well above our budget target, while also reducing our exploration costs. While growing upstream and ensuring steady results in our gas and power businesses and refining, we are also recording transition businesses results in excess of our expectations, increasingly contributing to the diversification of the Group's earnings. The capital raise and the new governance of Plenitude have further derisked Eni's balance sheet and will enable this satellite to grow in a self-funded way. Thanks to our key businesses, industrial execution and larger optionality, we are able to generate robust and recurring cash flows over wide market conditions. Based on those fundamentals and the revised FY '26 cash flow outlook and with proforma gearing at historic lows, we are further raising the distribution by an extraordinary dividend of €0.46 per share to be paid in 4Q and increasing again the buyback to €3.8 bln, underscoring our continued commitment to sharing upside with shareholders." In Argentina, the consortium YPF/Eni/XRG engaged in developing a massive integrated LNG project with a target export capacity of initial 12 mln tonnes/y, has applied for the Country's fiscal regime to incentivize large investments (RIGI), marking a milestone towards the project's FID, expected around the end of this year. New access via the farm-in of an exploration block off Uruguay and signed agreement for an adjacent block. Also awarded the Sapukala block with 100% participating interest in the Kutei Basin, in Indonesia, within the framework of the first Indonesia Petroleum Bidding Round 2026. Continuing momentum at transition businesses translated into outperformance and increasing contribution to Group's earnings Enilive and Plenitude delivered adjusted EBITDA of €1.8 bln in the nine-month '26 (+20% vs last year). Completed the agreement with Ares to establish joint control over Plenitude by means of a non-proportional share capital increase diluting Eni's interest from 70% to 65%. With the new governance, this resulted in the deconsolidation of the entity from Eni's balance sheet as of end of September '26. Thanks to the new structure, Plenitude can pursue efficient, self-funded sustainable long-term growth and is on track to reach the targeted installed capacity of 6.5 GW at year-end. Enilive delivered strong results thanks to better biorefining margin environment, again confirming the importance of this emerging new source of clean fuel for transportation. Portfolio management as a tool to anticipate asset monetization and to obtain an optimal risk-reward profile of the business Closed a long-term partnership agreement with private equity investors relating to an infrastructural based E&P portfolio in exchange for a €1.8 bln capital contribution cashed in the quarter, which will be invested in higher-return projects. Completed the divestment of a 10% interest in the Baleine oilfield to Socar in line with our Dual-Exploration model. In October signed binding agreements to farm out Eni's retained 10% w.i. in two pools of Indonesian gas assets in the Kutei Basin to Pertamina and Medco, comprising the two giant development projects of the Northern and Southern hubs and the Geliga discovery. Improved performance at the Transformation businesses Better plant utilization rates in a favorable scenario drove a strong quarter at the Refining business. Reconversion to the transition of the main Versalis hubs of Brindisi/Priolo is progressing, while the scenario in the petrochemicals legacy business has taken another leg down. Excellent 3Q results driven by volume growth, plant reliability and portfolio optimizations enabled the Company to fully capture the scenario upside. Strategic developments In an exceptional year for exploration, discovered resources in the nine months '26 stand at 1.23 bln boe from activities in Angola, Namibia, C&ocirc;te d'Ivoire, Libya, Egypt, Norway and Indonesia. In August, Argentina LNG, the integrated liquefied natural gas (LNG) project promoted by YPF, Eni and XRG, has submitted its application to join Argentina's Large Investment Incentive Regime (RIGI), supporting the development of a large-scale LNG export project leveraging the resources of the Vaca Muerta formation. The submission of the RIGI application represents an important milestone toward the project's Final Investment Decision (FID) targeted by the end of 2026. In August, Eni and Sonatrach signed a new Memorandum of Intent aimed at expanding cooperation between the two companies in the promotion of concrete initiatives for the decarbonisation of the Oil & Gas sector and the reduction of emissions in Algeria, while adopting international best practices. In particular, the parties will cooperate in the field of natural CO₂ removal initiatives through forestry projects and will strengthen activities for the monitoring, quantification and reduction of methane emissions through the adoption of relevant international Oil & Gas standards. In August, Eni signed with MIWEN (a wholly owned subsidiary of YPF) and the Uruguayan National Oil Company ANCAP, the agreement for entering the OFF-5 exploration block offshore Uruguay, following approval by the Uruguayan authorities. Eni is the operator of the OFF-5 block with a 50% interest. Eni's entry into Uruguay alongside MIWEN further strengthens the collaboration between Eni and YPF, partners in the integrated upstream-midstream Argentina LNG (ARGLNG) project, as well as Eni's presence in South America. In September, Eni signed the "Contrato de Participación Productiva de Hidrocarburos" (CPPH) for the development of the Junín 5 giant oil field, located onshore in the Orinoco Belt. The signing of the CPPH marks the completion of the process launched with the signing of the Head of Terms in April. The 25-year agreement, with the possibility of extension, grants Eni the role of exclusive operator of the Junín 5 area, with full responsibility for the technical, financial and commercial management of the project. Junín 5 is a heavy oil field containing 35 bln bbl of certified oil in place. It currently produces approximately 12 kbbl/d. In September, Eni announced the completion of the sale of a 10% stake in the Baleine project in C&ocirc;te d'Ivoire to SOCAR. The transaction aligns with Eni's strategy of optimising its upstream portfolio through its Dual Exploration approach. In September, Eni was awarded a new exploration licence on the Sapukala block in the prolific Kutei Basin, Indonesia. In October, Eni signed two binding agreements to sell interests in certain Indonesian upstream assets operated by Searah, which comprised a 10% interest in the East Sepinggan, North Ganal, and Rapak PSCs to Pertamina, Indonesia's national energy company and a 10% interest in the West Ganal, East Ganal, Ganal and Muara Bakau PSCs to Medco, a Southeast Asian energy and natural resources company. These transactions help to further confirm the material value created by Eni through the operation of M&A, exploration and project development.
